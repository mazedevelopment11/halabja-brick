01 — About the Chain

Three factories, one legacy.

The Halabja Group Brick Factory Chain unites three independent production sites — Aso Brick, Hilal Brick, and Ishtar Brick — under a single standard of quality, engineering, and reliability.

Together they form one of the region's most consistent supplies of structural and facing brick, serving contractors, developers, and builders across Halabja and the wider Kurdistan Region. Each factory operates its own kilns and quality lines, while sharing the group's commitment to durable, precisely fired clay brick built to last generations.
