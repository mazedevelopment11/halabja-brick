About Hilal Brick

Built big, built clean.

Hilal Brick Factory is a Halabja Group company covering 180,000 m² — which the group describes as the biggest brick factory in the Middle East.

The plant runs on the latest European production technology and follows international standards. It uses high-quality, salt-free raw material, re-uses rainwater and recycles the heat from the process to dry bricks, reducing CO₂ production.

Hilal bricks provide great thermal insulation, are fire-proof and have strong sound insulation — available in four types: A, D, E and F.
