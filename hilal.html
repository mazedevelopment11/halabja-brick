About Hilal Brick

Refined finishes, built to last.

Hilal Brick is the facing and finishing specialist of the Halabja Group Brick Factory Chain, firing clay brick that's meant to be seen — not just built on top of.

Color consistency across a pallet, sharp edges, and a weather-resistant surface are the difference between a façade that ages well and one that doesn't. Hilal's kiln lines are tuned specifically for that kind of finish work, batch after batch.

For architects and builders who care how a wall reads from the street, Hilal Brick is the chain's answer — durability with a refined face.
