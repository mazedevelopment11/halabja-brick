About Ishtar Brick

Signature brick, made to order.

Ishtar Brick is the specialty arm of the Halabja Group Brick Factory Chain, built for projects that call for something beyond a standard catalog brick.

Custom firing profiles, textured surfaces, and heritage-style finishes let architects specify a look that's distinct to a single project, without giving up the durability of fired clay. It's slower, more deliberate production — sized to the project, not the pallet.

When a build needs a brick that doesn't look like everyone else's, Ishtar Brick is where that conversation starts.
