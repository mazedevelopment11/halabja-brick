About Aso Brick

Structural strength, at scale.

Aso Brick is the structural anchor of the Halabja Group Brick Factory Chain — a high-capacity production site built around consistency, load-bearing strength, and dependable supply for large-scale construction.

Every batch runs through the same kiln discipline and dimensional checks, so a pallet delivered today matches the one delivered last month. That reliability is what contractors lean on when they're sequencing a build around brick deliveries, not working around them.

From foundations to full structural walls, Aso Brick supplies the load-bearing backbone that the rest of a build sits on — engineered for strength first, finish second.
