About Aso Brick

Structural strength, at scale.

Aso Brick Factory is part of Halabja Group, producing a full range of clay bricks — ten types for load-bearing and non-load-bearing walls — for residential towers, housing developments and schools across Iraq.

Every brick follows the same production line: raw material preparation, crushing and grinding, mixing, shaping, drying, firing at around 1,100–1,200 °C, cooling, quality control and distribution. Finished bricks are inspected for defects, size, color and strength before they leave the yard.

Aso Brick has supplied projects such as Garden City Towers, Empire Diamond, Garden City Kirkuk and Garden City Ramadi.

Brick Types

Ten types. One standard.

Type F

Load bearing

One of the most prevalent bricks in the Kurdistan region, widely used for its ability to bear heavy loads. It also retains heat, acting as a thermal insulator for interior and exterior construction.

Dimensions20 × 20 × 40 cm
Weight14.2 kg

Type A

Load bearing

One of the most widely used types of bricks, valued for its high quality and durability.

Dimensions24 × 7.5 × 11.5 cm
Weight2.38 kg

Type G

A versatile, lightweight building block that is easy to use, with excellent thermal and sound insulation. Fire resistant, robust, environmentally friendly and earthquake resistant.

Dimensions20 × 20 × 40 cm
Weight14.45 kg

Type J

Load bearing

A distinctive Aso type that withstands high pressure (weight 15–16 kg). Used in walls that bear heavy loads and do not require the support of columns.

Dimensions24 × 20 × 40 cm
Weight15.9 kg

Type C

Non-load bearing

A slimmer block with low K-value, making it energy efficient. Weighs 5.4–5.7 kg and is used for interior partition walls.

Dimensions10 × 20 × 40 cm
Weight5.6 kg

Type D

Non-load bearing

Requires columns as it does not bear loads, but conserves energy very well — helping the house stay warm in winter and moderate in summer.

Dimensions15 × 20 × 40 cm
Weight8.1 kg

Type E

Non-load bearing

Prevents energy loss very well, keeping homes warm in winter and cool in summer. Used for partitions and sound insulation; compact size makes it fast to lay.

Dimensions20 × 20 × 40 cm
Weight9.95 kg

Type H

Load bearing

A distinguished Aso type with different dimensions and the ability to withstand pressure. Weighs 5.3–5.7 kg.

Dimensions18.7 × 24 × 14.3 cm
Weight5.5 kg

Type K

Non-load bearing

Does not bear heavy loads (requires columns) and is a good insulator for sound and heat. Weighs 12–12.5 kg.

Dimensions24 × 20 × 40 cm
Weight12.5 kg

Type I

Load bearing

A strong, durable brick that resists compression (10.4–10.7 kg). Used for load-bearing walls or buildings that do not have columns.

Dimensions15 × 20 × 40 cm
Weight10.6 kg
